### `python/ferrum/routing.py` (new)
- `PoolConfig` frozen dataclass mirroring `Connection` pool kwargs (independently tuned pools).
- `ConnectionRegistry(configs, *, parallelism=8)`:
  - Rejects non-PostgreSQL DSNs at construction (`_ensure_postgres_dsn`) — structural enforcement
    of "PostgreSQL-only, no dialect-switching Session".
  - Rejects empty configs, `parallelism < 1`, and empty shard names.
  - `start()` opens all pools with bounded `asyncio.Semaphore`; on any open failure, already-opened
    pools are closed (no partial registry left behind).
  - `get(name)` raises `FerrumConfigError` for unknown/not-started/closed shards.
  - `health_check(timeout=)` probes every pool concurrently; a failing pool is reported `False`
    without aborting the rest.
  - `close()` idempotent; bounded parallel close; clears connections.
  - `stats()` returns per-shard `PoolStats | None`.
  - `names` property.
- `ShardRouter(Generic[ShardKeyT])`:
  - Wraps a registry + caller-supplied `resolver: Callable[[ShardKeyT], str]`. The resolver is the
    single place routing policy lives; the router never inspects model metadata, tenant ids, or
    schema names to pick a connection.
  - `connection_for(shard_key)` resolves the trusted key to a registered `Connection`.
  - `transaction_for(shard_key, ...)` opens a `Connection.transaction()` on the resolved shard and
    yields the `Transaction` (QuerySet stays shard-unaware; caller passes it explicitly).
  - `stats()`/`health_check()` delegate to the registry.
- No `platform_scoped` model flag. No implicit QuerySet connection selection. mysql/sqlite/mssql
  extras are out of scope (rejected at registration).
